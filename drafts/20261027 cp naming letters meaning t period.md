# why is letter t used for repetition period

The letter **T** is used to represent the repetition period because **"period" is fundamentally a measurement of time**. [](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/28307/why-is-the-period-marked-as-letter-t)

The lowercase letter _**t**_ is traditionally reserved as the variable for _continuous, generic time_ in math and physics formulas. Because the repetition period is a highly specific, fixed duration of time—specifically, the time it takes for a cyclic phenomenon to complete one full, repeating cycle—scientists used the **capitalized letter _T_** to distinguish it from generic time. [](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/28307/why-is-the-period-marked-as-letter-t)

### Why "T" and Not Another Letter?

* **Etymology:** Both the english word _time_ and the variable _T_ trace back roots to the Latin word for time, _**tempus**_. [](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/28307/why-is-the-period-marked-as-letter-t)
* **Distinction in Equations:** If a wave is moving or a system is oscillating over time, you often need to calculate where it is at any random moment (_t_) versus how long it takes to complete a whole loop (_T_). Using **_t_ for instantaneous time** and **_T_ for the period** allows both variables to live in the same equation without confusion. [](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/28307/why-is-the-period-marked-as-letter-t)
* **Reciprocal of Frequency:** A repetition period is the inverse of frequency (T = 1/f). While frequency measures _how often_ something repeats per second, the period _T_ measures the actual _seconds per cycle_. [](https://www.britannica.com/science/period-physics)

### Common Industrial Applications

You will see this notation across many fields:

* **Physics & Electronics:** The time a repeating wave takes to travel from one crest to the next.
* **Signal Processing (Digital Audio/Video):** The sampling period or the exact time interval at which a signal is repeatedly measured.
* **Medical Imaging:** In MRI scans, the variable **TR** explicitly stands for [Repetition Time](https://mriquestions.com/tr-and-te.html), denoting the exact period between consecutive radiofrequency pulse cycles. [](https://mriquestions.com/tr-and-te.html)
