how to write in title case

Title case means capitalizing the first letter of most words in a title. The exact rules depend on the style guide you follow (like APA, MLA, or Chicago), but the general rules are highly consistent.

### Core Rules

- **Capitalize** the first and last words of the title.
- **Capitalize** all nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs.
- **Lowercase** articles (_a, an, the_).
- **Lowercase** coordinating conjunctions (_and, but, for, or, nor_).
- **Lowercase** short prepositions (_in, on, at, to, by, for_).

### Example Transformation

- **Incorrect**: how to write a book in the summer
- **Correct**: How to Write a Book in the Summer

### Style Guide Differences

- **APA & Chicago**: Lowercase all prepositions, regardless of length (_with, about, through_).
- **MLA**: Capitalize prepositions that are five letters or longer (_Through, About_).
