# compare sample and example

The core difference between **sample** and **example** lies in their purpose and how they represent a larger group. An **example is a model or illustration** used to explain an idea, while a **sample is a physical piece or data subset** used for testing, inspection, or analysis.

Here is a direct breakdown of how they compare:

| Feature              | Example                                                                   | Sample                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Purpose**  | To illustrate, clarify, or prove a point.                                 | To test, analyze, or represent a whole population.                            |
| **Nature**           | Can be abstract (an idea, a behavior, a math problem) or concrete.        | Usually a physical object, a product specimen, or a subset of data.           |
| **Selection Method** | Chosen intentionally because it perfectly embodies or typifies a concept. | Often chosen at random or through a specific scientific method to avoid bias. |
| **Fields of Use**    | Common in teaching, writing, everyday speech, and explanations.           | Common in statistics, science, medical testing, manufacturing, and commerce.  |

### When to Use "Example"

Use **example** when you want to show what something is like by providing a specific instance to help someone understand.

* _Sentence:_ "Can you give me an **example** of how to use this grammar rule?"
* _Sentence:_ "She is a prime **example** of a great leader."

### When to Use "Sample"

Use **sample** when you are dealing with a portion taken from a larger quantity to be tested, tasted, or studied.

* _Sentence:_ "The doctor took a blood **sample** to check for infections."
* _Sentence:_ "We surveyed a **sample** of 500 voters to predict the election outcome."
