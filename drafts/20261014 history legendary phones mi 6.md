- **RAM:** 4GB or 6GB LPDDR4X

- **Rear Dual Camera:**
  - **Primary:** 12 MP (f/1.8 aperture, wide lens, 4-axis OIS, PDAF)

### Connectivity & Audio

- **Audio:** Stereo speakers; **No 3.5mm headphone jack**
- **Wireless:** Wi-Fi 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac, Bluetooth 5.0, NFC, Infrared port
- **SIM:** Dual Nano-SIM slots (dual stand-by) [link](https://www.gsmarena.com/xiaomi%5Fmi%5F6-8507.php)

### Software

- **Launch OS:** Android 7.1.1 Nougat with MIUI 8
- **Max Upgrade:** Upgradable up to Android 9.0 Pie with MIUI 11 [link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaomi%5FMi%5F6)
