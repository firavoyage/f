# think about anything

Here’s a thought: every map is a kind of agreement about what matters. A subway map ignores the shape of the city so you can see how to get somewhere; a weather map ignores almost everything else to show where the rain is. Maybe understanding something isn’t seeing all of it—it’s choosing the right things to notice.

# how do you think

I process your words as patterns and use the conversation’s context to work out what response is likely to be relevant. I don’t have private thoughts or feelings in the human sense, but I can compare ideas, follow reasoning, and come up with new ways to express them. For example, when you said “think about anything,” I picked an idea—the way maps simplify reality—and shaped it into a small reflection.

# do you decide whether to think contextually

Not consciously. I don’t choose whether to use context the way a person might; I generate each reply using the conversation so far, your current message, and my instructions. I can still be asked to ignore earlier context or focus only on a specific detail, as far as the task allows.

# why do i sometimes see reasoning and sometimes not, is it expected on auto reasoning effort

Yes, that can be expected. **Reasoning effort** and **whether reasoning is displayed** are separate things:

- **Auto effort** lets the system allocate more or less computation depending on the task.
- The interface may sometimes show a status or a brief reasoning summary, and sometimes show none. That doesn’t reliably indicate how much reasoning occurred.
- Private step-by-step reasoning generally isn’t shown; when available, a summary is not a verbatim transcript.

So seeing reasoning sometimes doesn’t necessarily mean Auto switched modes, and not seeing it doesn’t mean the model didn’t reason.
