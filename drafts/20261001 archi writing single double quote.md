# when should single or double quote be used

The choice between single and double quotation marks <mark>depends entirely on<span> </span><strong>geographic style guides</strong><span> </span>and<span> </span><strong>nesting</strong></mark> (quotes within quotes). [link](https://www.scribbr.com/language-rules/quotation-marks/)

Here is the quick-scannable breakdown of how they are used in writing:

### 🇺🇸 American English Style

In the United States and Canada, **double quotation marks are the primary choice** for general writing, while single quotation marks are strictly reserved for internal nesting. [link](https://www.facebook.com/GrammarGirl/posts/do-you-know-when-to-use-quotation-marks-how-about-single-quotation-marks/10165682146720228/)

- **Double Quotes (")**: Used for direct speech, dialogue, and titles of short works (like songs or articles).
  - _Example:_ "I love that song," Sarah said. [link](https://wmich.edu/writing/punctuation/quotationmarks)
- **Single Quotes (')**: Used exclusively for a **quote within a quote**.
  - _Example:_ Tom said, "Sarah told me, **'I love that song,'** but I didn't believe her." [link](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/commonly-confused-words/single-vs-double-quotes/)

### 🇬🇧 British and Australian English Style

In the United Kingdom, Australia, and many other Commonwealth nations, the rule is **completely reversed**. [link](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/grammar-punctuation-and-conventions/punctuation/quotation-marks)

- **Single Quotes (')**: Used for standard direct speech, dialogue, and short titles.
  - _Example:_ 'I love that song,' Sarah said. [link](https://davidbrewsterwriter.com/blog/2014/8/19/quote-me-on-this-using-single-and-double-quotation-marks)
- **Double Quotes (")**: Used for a **quote within a quote**.
  - _Example:_ Tom said, 'Sarah told me, **"I love that song,"** but I didn't believe her.' [link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeOdzA6fy9A)

***

### Directly Comparing the Regional Rules

| Rule Category             | American English (US) 🇺🇸        | British English (UK) 🇬🇧                |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Quotes**        | **Double** (`"like this"`)      | **Single** (`'like this'`)             |
| **Nested Quotes**         | **Single** inside Double        | **Double** inside Single               |
| **Punctuation Placement** | Periods/commas go **inside**    | Periods/commas go **outside**          |
| **Headlines**             | **Single** quotes to save space | **Single** or double (varies by paper) |

### Specific Exceptions

1. **Newspaper Headlines**: Even in the US, mainstream news editors use **single quotes in headlines** to save horizontal spacing (e.g., _Mayor Says 'No' to New Taxes_). [link](https://emma-alpern-80872.medium.com/when-should-i-use-single-quotation-marks-cf1eb963a434)
2. **Coding**: If you are writing software (like Python or JavaScript), single and double quotes are often functionally identical for enclosing text strings, but they have distinct behavioral rules in environments like SQL or shell scripting. [link](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/MrRPo-roIyk)
