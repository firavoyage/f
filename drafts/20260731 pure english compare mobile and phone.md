compare mobile and phone

While often used interchangeably today, <mark>"phone" is a broad umbrella term, whereas "mobile" specifically refers to a cellular, portable device</mark>.

### Core Differences

- **Definition**: A **phone** is any device that transmits vocal audio over a distance \[The Oxford English Dictionary]. A **mobile** is a specific type of phone that connects via wireless cellular networks rather than physical wires.
- **Mobility**: Traditional phones (landlines) are tethered to a specific wall outlet. Mobile phones travel with you anywhere within network coverage.
- **Technology**: Landline phones use copper wires or fiber-optic cables (PSTN/VoIP). Mobile phones use radio waves to connect to local cellular towers.
- **Power Source**: Traditional phones draw power directly from the telephone line or a wall outlet. Mobile phones rely on rechargeable internal batteries.

### Feature Comparison

- **Features**: Traditional phones only handle voice calls and basic caller ID. Mobile phones support texting, internet browsing, apps, and photography.
- **Reliability**: Landline phones often work during electrical power outages. Mobile phones depend on local cell tower uptime and battery life.
- **Audio Quality**: Wired phones generally offer consistent, high-definition voice clarity. Mobile phone quality can drop based on signal strength and environmental interference.
