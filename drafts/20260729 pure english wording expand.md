verbs to describe to make sth more comprehensive

To make something more comprehensive, you can use powerful verbs like <mark><strong>expand, enrich, integrate, elaborate, or systematicize</strong></mark>, depending on whether you are adding data, combining ideas, or structuring information.

The best verbs to use are categorized below by their specific context.

### ➕ Adding Information and Detail

Use these verbs when you need to increase the volume of information, depth, or scope: [link](https://langeek.co/en/vocab/subcategory/4650/word-list)

- **Expand**: To add more details or sections to a topic.
- **Enrich**: To improve quality by adding rich, meaningful content.
- **Elaborate**: To provide additional, detailed explanations for existing ideas.
- **Flesh out**: To add substance to an incomplete outline or draft.
- **Augment**: To increase the size or value of something by adding to it. [link](https://langeek.co/en/vocab/subcategory/4650/word-list)

### 🔗 Combining and Unifying Elements

Use these verbs when a comprehensive state is achieved by bringing scattered pieces together: [link](https://www.englishgrammar.org/power-verbs/)

- **Integrate**: To combine diverse elements into a single, unified whole.
- **Consolidate**: To merge multiple sources of information into one definitive place.
- **Synthesize**: To combine different ideas to form a complex, complete understanding.
- **Amalgamate**: To blend distinct elements into a uniform project or text.
- **Centralize**: To bring data from different areas into one main hub. [link](https://www.usgs.gov/human-capital/measurable-performance-verbs-writing-objectives)

### 📐 Broadening the Scope

Use these verbs when you want to make something cover a wider range of topics or categories: [link](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/comprehensive)

- **Broaden**: To extend the boundaries of a study or conversation.
- **Diversify**: To add different types of ideas, examples, or views.
- **Encompass**: To expand a project so it includes everything required.
- **Extend**: To make a resource reach further or last longer. [link](https://reedsy.com/blog/list-strong-verbs/)

### 🗂 Organising and Structuring

Use these verbs when comprehensiveness comes from thorough, systematic organization: [link](https://www.englishgrammar.org/power-verbs/)

- **Systematize**: To organize facts or procedures into a logical, comprehensive system.
- **Itemize**: To list every single component individually so nothing is forgotten.
- **Codify**: To arrange rules, laws, or data into a systematic, complete code.
- **Catalog**: To build a complete, detailed list of available items or information. [link](https://management.buffalo.edu/content/dam/mgt/Career-Resources/Documents/ActionVerbs.pdf)
