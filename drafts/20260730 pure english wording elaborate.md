verbs to describe to make sth more detailed

The best verbs to use when you want to describe making something more detailed include **elaborate**, **expand**, **flesh out**, and **specify**. [link](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/73964/is-there-a-single-word-that-means-adding-more-details-or-enriching-with-infor)

The exact verb you should choose depends entirely on the context of what you are making more detailed. You can group these verbs into four distinct categories:

### Explaining or Writing

Use these verbs if you are adding more information to a statement, a story, or an explanation. [link](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/397550/what-is-a-long-complicated-word-for-adding-many-unnecessary-details-to-make-a-s)

- **Elaborate**: To provide more details or add information to something you have already said.
- **Expand**: To talk or write about a topic in greater depth, building on a basic idea.
- **Expound**: To give a very careful, structured, and highly detailed explanation of a complex theory or idea.
- **Explicate**: To analyze a piece of writing or an idea meticulously, explaining every single detail. [link](https://www.merriam-webster.com/thesaurus/explain)

### Developing Plans or Ideas

Use these verbs when you are taking a basic concept and making it fully formed or complete.

- **Flesh out**: To add substance, details, or specific features to a basic outline or a rough draft.
- **Develop**: To gradually work on something to make it more advanced, complex, or complete.
- **Enrich**: To improve the quality of something by adding more detailed information or valuable elements. [link](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/73964/is-there-a-single-word-that-means-adding-more-details-or-enriching-with-infor)

### Creating Precise Lists or Requirements

Use these verbs if you need to break information down into exact, individual components. [link](https://www.thesaurus.com/browse/more-detailed)

- **Specify**: To state explicitly, clearly, and precisely what is required.
- **Itemize**: To list individual items or details one by one.
- **Particularize**: To provide specific, individual details rather than speaking in general terms.
- **Enumerated**: To name a number of things one by one in a specific list. [link](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/73964/is-there-a-single-word-that-means-adding-more-details-or-enriching-with-infor)

### Refining and Tuning

Use these verbs if you are tweaking something that already exists to make its details more accurate. [link](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/263048/word-meaning-to-make-more-accurate)

- **Refine**: To make small, precise changes to improve the clarity or accuracy of an idea or a process.
- **Fine-tune**: To make very small, highly detailed adjustments to achieve top performance. [link](https://english.stackexchange.com/questions/263048/word-meaning-to-make-more-accurate)
