Here is a curated collection of mind-blowing facts spanning history, science, nature, and language.

### 🌌 Space & Earth

- **Trees outnumber stars**: Earth has roughly 3 trillion trees, while the Milky Way has only 100 to 400 billion stars.
- **Long days on Venus**: One single day on Venus lasts longer than an entire Venusian year.
- **Lunar golfing**: Golf was the very first sport ever played on the Moon.
- **Clockwise rotation**: Venus is the only planet in our solar system that spins clockwise.
- **Cosmic color**: The average color of the universe is a beige shade named "Cosmic Latte". [link](https://www.today.com/life/inspiration/interesting-facts-rcna130243)

### 🦖 History & Time

- **Cleopatra's timeline**: Cleopatra lived closer to the building of the iPhone than to the construction of the Great Pyramid of Giza.
- **Ancient school**: The University of Bologna in Italy is the world's oldest university, founded back in 1088.
- **A blink in time**: If Earth's history were compressed into 24 hours, humans have only existed for the last 2 to 3 seconds.
- **Secret tech origin**: The popular college party Jell-O shot was invented by a brilliant MIT mathematician in the 1950s.
- **Shortest conflict**: The Anglo-Zanzibar War of 1896 holds the record for the shortest war in history, lasting a mere 38 minutes. [link](https://www.today.com/life/inspiration/interesting-facts-rcna130243)

### 🥑 Nature & Food

- **Secret berries**: Bananas, pumpkins, watermelons, and avocados are all botanically classified as berries.
- **Imposter berries**: Strawberries, raspberries, and blackberries are not actually berries at all.
- **Massive clouds**: An average cumulus cloud weighs around 1.1 million pounds (about 500,000 kilograms).
- **Whale appetites**: A blue whale can consume nearly half a million calories in just one single mouthful.
- **Tree networks**: African acacia trees communicate with each other by releasing airborne gasses to warn neighboring trees of predators. [link](https://www.today.com/life/inspiration/interesting-facts-rcna130243)

### 💬 Language & Human Body

- **Unique prints**: Just like your fingerprints, every single person has a completely unique tongue print.
- **Silent movement**: When you read silently to yourself, your vocal cords and mouth muscles still make microscopic movements.
- **Letter scarcity**: The letter "J" is the only letter of the alphabet that does not appear anywhere on the periodic table.
- **Bone fusion**: Human babies are born with around 270 bones, but many fuse together to leave just 206 by adulthood.
- **Unique ending**: "Dreamt" is the only standard English word that ends with the letters "mt". [link](https://www.niehs.nih.gov/health/kids/games/riddles/jokes/fun-facts-and-trivia)
