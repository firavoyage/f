### 🔄 The Transition

When Tencent initially launched _PUBG Mobile_ in China as a beta test, they were unable to secure a monetization license from the Chinese government due to strict regulations against in-game violence. Because Tencent could not sell in-game items or subscriptions to its 70 million active Chinese users, they made a strategic shift in May 2019:

- **The Shutdown:** Tencent completely shut down the _PUBG Mobile_ testing servers in China.
- **The Migration:** They immediately migrated the entire player base, including user accounts, levels, and unlocked items, to a brand-new, regulatory-compliant title: **Heping Jingying**. [link](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/pubg-bloodless-patriotic-facelift-china)

***

### ⚖️ Key Differences Between the Two Games

While the gameplay loop remains a 100-player battle royale, _Heping Jingying_ was given a "patriotic facelift" to appease Chinese censors and secure a license to make money. [link](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/asia-pacific/tencent-plays-safe-over-china-pubg-launch/)

| Feature              | PUBG Mobile (Global)                                                  | Heping Jingying (China)                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Theme**            | Survival-focused gritty tactical battle royale.                       | Anti-terrorism military training exercise that pays tribute to the People's Liberation Army Air Force.                          |
| **Violence & Gore**  | Features blood splatter effects and realistic death animations.       | Completely bloodless. Defeated players do not die; instead, they sit up, wave goodbye, hand over their loot box, and disappear. |

| **Server Ecosystem** | Connected to global regional servers (NA, EU, Asia, etc.).            | Strictly locked to a domestic Chinese server ecosystem requiring Chinese social login credentials (WeChat or QQ).               |

The two games are closely tied in the competitive esports scene. China’s premier professional league for _Heping Jingying_, known as the **PEL (Peacekeeper Elite League)**, serves as the direct qualification pathway for Chinese teams to compete against international players in global _PUBG Mobile_ tournaments, such as the _PUBG Mobile Global Championship (PMGC)_. When Chinese teams qualify, they easily transition to the global client for the tournament.
