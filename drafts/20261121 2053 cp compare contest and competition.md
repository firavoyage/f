# compare contest and competition

**Contest** and **competition** both involve people or groups trying to win, but they’re used somewhat differently.

- **Contest** usually means a specific event or challenge with a winner:  
  *a spelling contest, a photo contest, a political contest*
- **Competition** can mean an event, but more often refers to the broader process or condition of trying to outperform others:  
  *competition between companies; competition for a job*

They can overlap: *a dance contest* and *a dance competition* are both natural. **Contest** can also mean to challenge or dispute something (*contest a decision*); **competition** cannot be used that way.
