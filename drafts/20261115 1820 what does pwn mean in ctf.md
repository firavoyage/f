# what does pwn mean in ctf

## what does pwn mean in ctf

In a CTF, **pwn** is shorthand for **“own”**—a category of challenges about exploiting software, usually a compiled program.

You’re typically given a binary and try to find a vulnerability, such as a buffer overflow or format-string bug, to make it behave in a way the author didn’t intend—often to read a secret or get the flag. It’s also called **binary exploitation**.

## why is it called

**“Pwn”** started as a misspelling of **“own,”** likely because **P** is next to **O** on a keyboard. In gaming and hacker slang, to “pwn” someone meant to beat or take control of them. In CTFs, **pwn** refers to exploiting a program—essentially, “owning” it.
