# flop meaning

The word **flop** primarily means **to fail completely** or **to fall, drop, or hang heavily and loosely**. Depending on the context, it serves as a verb, a noun, or a specialized term in fields like sports, gaming, and computing. [](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/flop)

### Everyday Meanings

* **To fail completely (Verb/Noun).** According to [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/flop), an event, movie, product, or social media post that performs poorly is called a flop. For example, you might say a big-budget movie was a box office flop. [](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/flop)
* **To drop or collapse heavily (Verb).** It describes sitting, lying, or falling down suddenly in an exhausted or clumsy way. You might flop onto the couch after a long day. [](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/flop)
* **To hang or swing loosely (Verb).** Something that lacks stiffness and moves awkwardly is said to flop. Examples include a dog's ears or a wide-brimmed hat flopping in the wind. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQ8k0li4Idc)
* **A place to sleep (Slang Noun).** As noted by [Collins Dictionary](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/flop), it can informally mean a cheap, temporary place to crash or sleep for the night. [](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/flop)

### Specialized & Context-Specific Meanings

* **Sports.** In basketball and soccer, flopping is when a player intentionally falls or exaggerates contact to trick a referee into calling a foul. In track and field, the "Fosbury Flop" is the standard backward-jumping technique used in the high jump.
* **Poker.** As explained on [Dictionary.com](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/flop), "the flop" refers to the first three community cards dealt face-up in the center of the table in games like Texas Hold'em.
* **Computing.** FLOP stands for **fl**oating-point **op**eration. Computing speed is often measured in FLOPS (floating-point operations per second), such as teraflops. [](https://www.dictionary.com/browse/flop)
