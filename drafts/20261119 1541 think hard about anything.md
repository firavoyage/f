# think hard about anything

A useful thing to think hard about is how much of a life is shaped by what we repeatedly notice.

Attention seems passive—we imagine the world arrives first, and then we look at it. But over time, the direction of our attention helps decide what the world becomes *for us*. Notice every slight, and the world fills with threats. Notice opportunities to help, and it fills with people who might need you. Notice small pleasures, and ordinary days become less empty.

This doesn’t mean we can simply choose to feel differently, or that hardship is a failure of perspective. It means attention is one of the few ways we participate in making our experience. So a quiet question worth asking is: **What am I practicing noticing?**
