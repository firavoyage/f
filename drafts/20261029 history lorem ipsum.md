# how is lorem ipsum created

**Lorem Ipsum was created by taking a 1st-century BC Latin philosophical text, splitting the words across pages, and scrambling the vocabulary into nonsensical gibberish**. [](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum)

While a popular myth claims it was invented by a 16th-century printer, historical evidence reveals that the modern text we use today was actually created in the **1960s**. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CmzKQ3PSrow&t=39)

### 1\. The Source Material (45 BC)

The text is derived from a treatise on ethics written by the Roman statesman and philosopher **Cicero**, titled _**De finibus bonorum et malorum**_ ("On the Ends of Good and Evil"). Specifically, it pulls from a section discussing the philosophy of pleasure and pain. [](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Lorem-ipsum)

The opening phrase _"Lorem ipsum..."_ comes from the Latin word _**dolorem ipsum**_, which translates to _"pain itself"_. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CmzKQ3PSrow&t=39)

### 2\. The Accidental Cut-Off (1914)

In 1914, the standard English-Latin edition of Cicero's work was published in the **Loeb Classical Library**. In this specific print edition, page 34 ended right in the middle of a word: [](https://fedtechmagazine.com/article/2021/06/history-lorem-ipsum)

* It broke off at: `Neque porro quisquam est qui do-`
* It picked up on page 36 with: `lorem ipsum...` [](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum)

### 3\. The 1960s Scrambling

In the 1960s, a British typography company called **Letraset** manufactured adhesive, rub-down transfer sheets used by graphic designers to build page layouts. To give designers a realistic text layout that wouldn't distract clients with readable content, Letraset typesetters copied sections out of that 1914 Loeb book. They deliberately chopped up, deleted, and rearranged Cicero's sentences to ensure the final product was completely unreadable. [](https://www.wix.com/studio/blog/generative-ai-lorem-ipsum)

### 4\. Digital Mass Adoption (1980s–Present)

In the 1980s, a software company called Aldus introduced **PageMaker**, the world’s first major desktop publishing program. They preloaded Letraset's scrambled _Lorem Ipsum_ text into the software as the default placeholder text. This established it as the universal tech industry standard, eventually leading to its integration into Microsoft Word, WordPress, and code-based web generators. [](https://fedtechmagazine.com/article/2021/06/history-lorem-ipsum)

# history of lorem ipsum

The history of **Lorem Ipsum** spans over **two millennia**, originating from a classical Latin treatise written by the Roman statesman and philosopher **Marcus Tullius Cicero** in **45 BC**. While popular myth often attributes its scrambling to a 16th-century printer, recent findings trace its actual design-world emergence to the mid-20th century. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CmzKQ3PSrow&t=39)

Here is the breakdown of how a philosophical essay on ethics became the global design industry standard for placeholder text.

### 🏛️ The Ancient Source (45 BC)

The text is derived from sections 1.10.32 and 1.10.33 of Cicero's philosophical work _De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum_ (_On the Ends of Good and Evil_). Written as a discourse on Epicurean ethics and hedonism, the original passage explores the relationship between pain and pleasure. [](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum)

The opening words, "_Lorem ipsum..._", come from "_**dolorem ipsum**_," which translates literally to **"pain itself"**. The original untamed sentence reads: [](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbCP2ifP9C%5F/?hl=en)

> _"Neque porro quisquam est, qui **dolorem ipsum**, quia **dolor sit, amet, consectetur, adipisci velit**..."_  
> _(Translation: "Nor is there anyone who loves pain itself, who seeks after it and wants to have it, simply because it is pain...")_

### 📖 The Modern Genesis (1914)

For a long time, people believed a Renaissance-era typesetter randomly mashed the words together. However, historical tracking shows that our exact modern layout likely stems from the **1914 Loeb Classical Library edition** of _De Finibus_. [](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum)

In that specific printing, the Latin text on page 34 awkwardly breaks off right at _"qui do-"_ and resumes on page 36 with _"lorem ipsum..."_. The subsequent scrambled phrasing matches the structural layouts of pages 56, 70, and 118 of that same book, suggesting someone used this specific edition to construct a block of nonsense text. [](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum)

### 💻 Commercialization & Digital Boom (1960s–1980s)

* **The 1960s:** The British company [Letraset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum) popularized the text by featuring Lorem Ipsum on its dry-transfer sheets. Graphic designers rubbed these adhesive sheets onto layouts to mimic realistic body copy before final text was written. [](https://fedtechmagazine.com/article/2021/06/history-lorem-ipsum)
* **The 1980s:** The software company **Aldus Corporation** integrated Lorem Ipsum into its groundbreaking desktop publishing software, PageMaker, as the default filler text. When PageMaker took off on early Apple Mac computers, Lorem Ipsum became permanently cemented in digital design culture. [](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kL1PDqzqhM4)

### 🔍 Uncovering the Mystery (1980s–1990s)

Despite its ubiquity, digital layout professionals didn't actually know where the text came from. Before 1982, **Richard McClintock**, a Latin scholar at Hampden–Sydney College, grew curious about the word _consectetur_—a rarely used word in classical literature. He searched for it across a physical database of classical Latin texts and successfully tied the jumbled boilerplate back to Cicero's original work. [](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorem%5Fipsum)

### Why We Still Use It Today

Designers use Lorem Ipsum because it mimics a **natural distribution of English letters** (using words of varying lengths, spaces, and punctuation). If a designer uses repetitive filler like _"text here, text here,"_ or real readable English, clients naturally stop focusing on visual layout elements—like fonts, margins, alignment, and kerning—and start reading the text instead. Lorem Ipsum acts as a visual anchor that communicates nothing, ensuring everyone stays focused on the design. [](https://priceonomics.com/the-history-of-lorem-ipsum/)
