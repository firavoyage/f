# 神棍 meaning

In Chinese, **“神棍” (shén gùn)** is a derogatory term that means a **charlatan, quack, or religious fraudster**. It refers to someone who claims to have supernatural powers or uses the guise of spirits, gods, and religion to trick people out of their money. [](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%A5%9E%E6%A3%8D)

### Breakdown of the Characters

* **神 (shén):** God, deity, spirit, or supernatural.
* **棍 (gùn):** Literally means "stick" or "staff", but in historical Chinese slang (dating back to the Ming and Qing dynasties), it was used to describe scoundrels, rascals, or swindlers (e.g., 光棍, 地棍). [](https://baike.baidu.com/ja/item/%E3%83%9A%E3%83%86%E3%83%B3%E5%B8%AB/1119035)

### Modern Usage & Context

While it originally described fake Taoist priests, fortune tellers, or spiritual mediums, the word has expanded in modern pop culture:

* **Literal Fraud:** Used for fake cult leaders, scammer psychics, or corrupt religious figures who exploit vulnerable people. [](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%A5%9E%E6%A3%8D)
* **Anime, Gaming, & Fiction:** It is often used affectionately or mockingly to describe characters who are overly mysterious, always speak in riddles, act like they can predict the future, or possess ridiculously overpowered "god-like" plot armor (e.g., characters who pull off impossible plans as if they are mind-readers).
