# synonyms of wonder noun

**Wonder** as a noun can mean:

- **Amazement:** astonishment, awe, surprise, admiration  
- **Something extraordinary:** marvel, miracle, phenomenon, sensation  
- **Curiosity:** curiosity, inquisitiveness  

The best synonym depends on the sentence.

# words like questions, puzzles, or confusions, like when you wonder how a problem can be solved, but emphasize curiosity rather than confusion

For that sense, these fit best:

- **Inquiry** — a question or investigation driven by curiosity  
- **Question** — neutral and direct  
- **Pondering** / **reflection** — the act of thinking curiously about something  
- **Puzzle** — a problem that invites thought or discovery  
- **Mystery** — something intriguing that you want to understand  

**Inquiry** is probably the closest if you want to emphasize curiosity rather than confusion. For example: *“Her inquiry was how the problem could be solved.”*

# to be plural and to describe a list of ideas

**Wonderings** is likely the word you want: it means questions or ideas that arise from curiosity.

- *A list of wonderings about how the problem could be solved*
- *The students shared their wonderings.*

Other options include **inquiries** (more formal) and **curiosities** (more like topics that interest you).

# can wondering be noun

Yes. **Wondering** can be a noun, usually referring to the act of thinking curiously: *“Her wondering led to a new idea.”*

The plural **wonderings** is also used for curiosity-driven questions or thoughts, especially in educational or reflective writing: *“We shared our wonderings about how the problem could be solved.”* It’s understandable, though less common than **questions** or **inquiries**.

# compare inquiries wonderings and curiosities

- **Inquiries** — specific questions or investigations, often with a formal or purposeful tone.  
  *“The team listed its inquiries about possible solutions.”*

- **Wonderings** — questions or thoughts sparked by curiosity; reflective and informal, often used in classrooms or brainstorming.  
  *“We shared our wonderings about how the problem might be solved.”*

- **Curiosities** — things that interest or intrigue you, rather than the questions themselves. It can also mean unusual or strange things.  
  *“Her curiosities included how the system worked.”*

For a list of curiosity-driven questions or ideas, **wonderings** is the closest fit; **inquiries** sounds more formal.
