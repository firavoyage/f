# info

you may export your submissions (not local files) after contest, when they publish it ("dom judge") on open web.

the contest paper and editorial (compiled from latex) are public. though they might not be good tutorials.

you may upsolve the problems. on the cf upsolving vp, seems you can view others' submissions.

some pros provide some one shot solutions for easy problems, excluding the (also relatively easy) ones of no attempts.
